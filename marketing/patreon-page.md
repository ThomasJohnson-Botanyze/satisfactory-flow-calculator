## Setup notes

- **Membership type:** monthly. Per-creation only makes sense if you post discrete "creations" — you don't.
- **Currency/price:** $3 / $5 / $10 are starting points. Satisfactory tool audiences skew global; $5 is a comfortable mid-tier.
- **Gate the exe with a patrons-only post.** Patreon lets you attach files visible only to patrons — host the `.exe` there and link it from the download page.
- **Before going live:** confirm Coffee Stain's fan-content / monetization stance. Framing matters — you're funding development of a free fan tool, not selling their IP. Donations sit safer than sales.
- **Link it everywhere:** in-app ♥ Support button (done), the download page, your Reddit post, Discord, site footer.
